VALIDATION RESULTS

The question

How can a simple classifier identify fraudulent transactions while balancing missed fraud and false alarms?

The approach

Seven transaction features feed a standardized logistic regression pipeline. Stratified sampling separates the data into 60% training, 20% validation, and 20% test sets.

A majority-class baseline provides context: it reaches 91.3% validation accuracy, yet detects no fraud. Precision and recall tell a more useful story.
